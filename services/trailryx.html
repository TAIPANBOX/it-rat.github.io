the record · Rust

Trailryx. A record of what your agents did that nobody can quietly change, or quietly shorten.

Trailryx answers with a completeness proof when it can, and says partial with the reason named when it cannot. And its store is built to erase one person on request without breaking the trail; the shipped node does not expose that yet.

two questions, two kinds of answer

The auditor asks. One answer carries a proof, the other admits it cannot.

A question on one of the five provable dimensions is a contiguous range, so its answer carries a proof. A question off them still gets the right rows, marked partial.

under the hood

SQL does not become a hole in the proof model.

Every answer says how much of it is proved. SQL is a facade in the tree, not yet a port in the shipped node, which reads through read.
