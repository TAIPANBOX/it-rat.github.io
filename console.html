Open your console

Your console does not run here. It runs on your own infrastructure, next to your stack, which is why your runs, your spend and your identities never travel to us to be displayed. This page is only a doorway: it sends your browser to your own address and remembers it here for next time.

Usually the tunnel address your box listens on, reachable once your VPN is up. A single-box install answers over HTTPS on the name it was configured with, once the WireGuard config it issued you is connected; that name is also what your passkey is bound to. Include http:// or https:// to be certain; without one, a private address is assumed to be plain HTTP inside the tunnel.

On this browser
We never see your credentials. Signing in happens on your own console, not on this site, and this page has nowhere to send a password even if it asked for one. The address you type is kept in this browser and is never sent to us.
